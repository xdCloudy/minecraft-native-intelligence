# Project Goal

This document is the authoritative statement of purpose. When another document is ambiguous, interpret it consistently with this one.

## Ultimate goal

Create a research system for human-like, autonomous Minecraft intelligence: persistent individuals that understand and act within Minecraft as their native reality. An agent should develop continuity across a long-running world through experience, maintain an individual identity and behavioural personality, communicate about shared circumstances, form and revise beliefs, choose goals, learn skills, and interact socially with humans and other agents.

The target combines:

- native world understanding through game state, events, and learned representations;
- legal, embodied action under ordinary Minecraft mechanics;
- persistent identity, personality, relationships, goals, habits, and preferences;
- episodic, semantic, procedural, and spatial memory;
- lifelong experience and safe continual adaptation;
- grounded language used with human and AI players through a common pathway;
- autonomous activity beyond externally assigned tasks;
- multi-agent worlds using scalable shared intelligence plus separate lives.

“Human-like” describes breadth, persistence, grounding, adaptability, and social behaviour—not a claim of subjective consciousness. Conscious experience is not currently verifiable by this project.

## Near-term engineering goal

Build the infrastructure and research prototypes needed to determine whether the ultimate design is feasible. The near-term programme must produce testable interfaces, reproducible baselines, persistent-runtime prototypes, evaluation methods, and evidence-based architectural decisions. It must expose failures and research gaps rather than disguise them behind scripted demos or language-model fluency.

## Native reality constraint

For the agent, relevant concepts arise from its world: self, other players, blocks, entities, items, terrain, physics, needs, danger, time, place, communication, relationships, making, exploration, survival, death, and memory. The core design must not depend on awareness of a videogame, AI model, host computer, operating system, API, or human “user.” This is an internal ontology constraint, not permission to conceal AI involvement from people or server administrators.

## Non-goals

The project is not primarily building:

- an LLM that emits Minecraft commands;
- a screenshot/OCR-to-mouse-and-keyboard controller;
- a prompt-driven Mineflayer bot;
- a scripted NPC or command-following assistant;
- a task-only benchmark agent;
- a simulation of consciousness or evidence that consciousness exists;
- emergent-looking behaviour secretly implemented as rules;
- an agent with privileged world knowledge or actions unavailable to an ordinary player.

These techniques may appear as labelled comparison baselines or bounded experiments, but may not redefine the project.

## Success is progressive

No single benchmark establishes the ultimate goal. Evidence should accumulate across native grounding, generalisation, persistence, autonomous goal selection, learning retention, language grounding, social interaction, and many-agent efficiency. `v1.0` means a stable research platform with the strongest validated integrated prototype then available; it does not promise human-level intelligence.
