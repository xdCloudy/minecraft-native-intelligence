# Vision

Imagine joining a long-running world and meeting players with histories rather than sessions. One remembers the bridge you built together, another has learned safer routes through a cave, and a third has gradually become meticulous about storage because disorder repeatedly caused losses. They can plan, misunderstand, ask, revise beliefs, collaborate, separate, and resume unfinished lives after downtime.

Their competence may share a foundation, but their lived state does not. A new individual begins with common priors and then diverges through places visited, relationships formed, failures survived, skills practised, and goals chosen. The human player is another person in the world—not an architectural owner whose prompts define all purpose.

The engineering challenge is to make this continuity causal and inspectable: grounded observations influence decisions; actions have ordinary consequences; memories retain provenance and uncertainty; personality shifts behaviour rather than prose; conversation updates and is checked against experience; shared inference scales without merging identities.

The project advances by falsifiable prototypes, not by declaring this vision complete.
